# Story: from BRAND.md to a script that hooks

## The 6 parts (always)
1. **Hook (0-4 s)**: a dated, concrete moment of the viewer's life where the pain shows (« Mardi, dix-neuf heures. »).
   A day + an hour + an object. Never the brand, never a question about the product.
2. **Silent gag (2-3 s)**: no voice, the pain in pictures, cut every 0.25 s (time-lapse of hours, a cursor that
   blinks, a pile that grows). It is the first « wow » of the edit.
3. **Diagnosis**: 3 concrete pains in the viewer's words (from BRAND.md: H2s, FAQ, testimonials), one sentence each,
   each with an object that can slam (a field, a case, a phone, a calendar).
4. **Pivot on black (2-3 s)**: one short sentence or question in serif italic (« Et si vous arrêtiez d'écrire ? »).
   Music cuts to silence. This is where the film turns.
5. **Solution**: the brand name spoken, then the product doing the work on its REAL interface: 2-3 features max,
   each mapped to one of the 3 pains (rhymes), with one verbatim proof if allowed.
6. **Brand + promise + CTA**: logo, a two-part promise (« Le courtier parle. L'IA fait tout le reste. »), the button
   of the site's main CTA, the click, a hold of ≥ 2 s.

## Archetypes (pick the closest, then make it specific)
| business | hook moment | silent gag | pivot | rhymes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS / admin tool | evening at the desk after the last meeting | clock time-lapse + cursor in an empty field | « Et si … ? » | the hour 19:00 → 22:47 → 19:05 |
| vertical software (broker, doctor, builder, lawyer) | the moment the client leaves / the site closes | paper pile, forms multiplying | the job they love vs the typing they hate | the same form, ticked by hand → by itself |
| fintech / invoicing | the unpaid invoice on day 30 | reminders stacking, calendar pages flying | « Vous avez été payé. » | the amount, red → green |
| mobile app / consumer | a real-life micro-moment (queue, commute, 23:58) | thumb scrolling between 5 apps | one app does it | the same gesture, before / after |
| e-commerce / DTC | the product moment (unboxing, the morning ritual) | the problem object failing in close-ups | the product reveal on black | the ritual, before / after |
| agency / service | the client's brief chaos (notes, calls, versions) | versions v1…v12 flashing | « Un seul interlocuteur. » | the deadline date |
| health / care | the waiting time | clock + chairs + numbers called | the human at the center | the same hour, waiting → seen |
| AI product | the blank page / the repetitive task | the same action copy-pasted 20× | « Vous parlez. Elle écrit. » | the input (voice/text) → the output |

## Writing rules
- One idea per sentence, 4-12 words. Numbers in letters in the voice text. 170-180 words per minute.
- The viewer is the subject (« Votre », « Vous »), the brand arrives at ~45-55 % of the film.
- Concrete nouns the picture can show (a field, a phone, a stamp) beat abstractions (« efficacité »).
- One emotional word per beat for the serif (« conseil », « lundi », « parlez », « le reste. »).
- Two versions: one rational (pains → solution), one emotional (a character, a moment). The user picks.
- Tone from BRAND.md (« vous » vs « tu », sentence length); language of the site unless the user says otherwise.
